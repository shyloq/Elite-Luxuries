# Home and shop (index.html)

Scope: the single-page store, from the vault entry through the shop, product view, bag and checkout. Mode: Persuade.

Audience: streetwear buyers who follow drops, mostly on phones. Job: get in, understand the label, buy from the current drop before sizes run out. Primary action: open a piece and add it to the bag. Proof on hand: real price, run size and units left per piece from Medusa; no photos, so garments are drawn placeholders.

Constraints: keep the vault gate (combination shown on screen), shop grid, product view with sizes and stock, bag and checkout working. No invented history, customer counts, contact details or services. First thing after the vault is a brand statement, then the shop.

Unresolved: which pieces are core line; real contact and social details; product photography.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the vault's security system. Visitors watch the vault on CCTV: the brand speaks as on-screen display text over a live feed, and the shop is the monitor wall. It refuses the category's hero-banner-over-card-grid store and the luxury serif look it replaces.

OWN-WORLD: Pale concrete grey ground; mid-grey camera feeds with live grain and faint scanlines; white on-screen display lettering with a hard black outline for camera labels, timestamps, prices and stock; a heavy, extra-wide grotesk for the wordmark and statements; ink near-black for body; one red recording dot as the only colour that is not grey. Garments show desaturated until a feed is selected.

STORY: The visitor turns the dial, lands on camera 01 watching the vault door, reads what VULT is in two lines, and sees the monitor wall begin below. Each feed shows a piece with its price and real units left; sold-out pieces lose signal. Selecting a feed takes it fullscreen, where they pick a size and add it to the bag.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A thin bar with the VULT LABEL wordmark left and DROP 03, INFO, BAG count right. Below it, camera 01 fills most of the screen: grain, a slow camera drift over the vault door, REC dot and CAM 01 top left, live date and time top right. The statement sits bottom left at display scale, MADE IN NUMBERED RUNS. LOCKED UNTIL THE DROP., with the primary action View Drop 03 beside it. The top edge of the monitor wall shows at the bottom of the screen.

FORM: Security feed, position 5 of 7 on the ordered list. Signature interaction: camera switching, where hovering or focusing a feed brings it live (colour returns, grain drops, white selection frame) and selecting it opens that camera fullscreen as the product view. Motion grammar: stepped, video-like timing; one scan roll on load; reduced motion removes drift, grain movement and the roll. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
